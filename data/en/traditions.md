# CATEGORY: chandramana_yugadi
## Chandramana Yugadi

Yugadi means 'beginning of the age' (complexity of the year). Chandramana Yugadi is celebrated based on the movement of the moon on Chaitra Shuddha Padya. There is a legend that Lord Brahma started the work of creation on this auspicious day.

On this day it is customary to taste neem-jaggery along with New Year celebrations. Bevu-bella symbolizes the life message of accepting happiness-sadness, hardship-happiness equally. Religiously on that day Abhyanga bath, panchanga sravana and special pooja to Shri God are prayed to bring prosperity and success in the new year.

# CATEGORY: sri_rama_navami
## Shri Ram Navami

On Chaitra Shuddha Navami, Sri Ramachandra, a virtuous man, was born. This is the day when Sriman Narayana became the son of Dasharatha Maharaja in Ayodhya as Rama avatar for the establishment of Dharma and killing of Ravana.

This day is celebrated with great fervour, to commemorate the ideals of Lord Rama who eradicated unrighteousness and upheld Dharma. Devotees observe a fast, offer special pooja to Lord Rama, recite Ramayana, offer panaka, kosambari and buttermilk and distribute it to the devotees.

# CATEGORY: chitra_paurnami
## Chitra Poornami

The full moon of Chaitra month is known as 'Chitra Pournami'. On this day, special worship is offered to Chitragupta, one of the Navagraha deities and Chitragupta, who writes accounts of Yamadharma.

It is believed that by worshiping Chitragupta, who keeps track of the sins and merits of human beings, unknowingly committed sins will be resolved and sanctioned. On this day water donation, food donation and special religious rituals are performed in temples.

# CATEGORY: nrusimha_jayanti
## Nrisimha Jayanti

On the evening (Pradosha kala) of Vaishakh Shukla Chaturdashi, Sri Hari emerged from the pillar as the fierce Narasimha to protect the devotee Prahlada and kill the demon Hiranyakashipu.

Celebrating Nrisimha Jayanti with great devotion will remove fear, enemy pain and all troubles. On that day there is fasting and in the evening Panchamritabhisheka, Tulsi offering and offerings are offered to Lord Nrisimha.

# CATEGORY: vaishakha_paurnami
## Vaishakh Poornami

The full moon of the Vaishakh month is religiously the most sacred day. Kurmavatara and Buddha Jayanti fall on the same day. In the month of Vaishakh, there is special significance in donating water, donating grains and donating umbrellas and shoes.

Bathing in holy rivers and worshiping Lord Lakshminarayan will fulfill all wishes. Special pooja, Abhishekam and Mahapooja are performed in temples.

# CATEGORY: rigupakarma
## Rigupakarma

Rigvedis celebrate 'Rigupakarma' (upakarma / thread full moon) on Sravana Nakshatra or Poornami in the month of Sravana. It is a sign of resumption of Vedadhyana.

On this day celibates and householders remove the old yajnopaveeta (Janivara) and wear new yajnopaveeta with mantra. The sage resolves to perform his sacred duties faithfully by doing tarpana, chanting Gayatri and remembering Veda Vyasa.

# CATEGORY: varamahalakshmi_vrata
## Varamahalakshmi Vrata

Varamahalakshmi Vrata is celebrated on the Friday preceding the full moon of the month of Shravan. This vrat has the background of the story of Shri Mahalakshmi giving darshan in a dream in appreciation of the devotion of a concubine named Charumati.

Goddess Lakshmidevi of Mangaladayinia is worshiped by placing a kalash at home, decorating it with turmeric-kumkum, fruits and flowers. Muttaidei observe this vrat and tie Nompu Dara for the health, wealth and fortune of their family.

# CATEGORY: sri_ganesha_chaturthi
## Shri Ganesha Chaturthi

On the Chaturthi of Shuklapaksha in the month of Bhadrapada, the birthday of Lord Mahaganapati, the Destroyer of Disruption, the Enlightener of Wisdom, is celebrated as 'Shri Ganesha Chaturthi'.

**Mythological Background and Ritual:**
It is a mythological legend that Goddess Parvati created Ganapati from the clay of her body and worshiped him. On this day, an idol of Ganesha made of holy clay is installed in homes and in public, Shodashopachara Puja is performed with durva (grill), shami, patri and various kinds of offerings (especially modaka, kadubu).

**Religious Significance of the Area:**
On this day special Ranga Pooja, Ashtottar Shatakalashabhisheka and Maha Pooja will be performed to Adipujita Shri Ganapati who is worthy of the first pooja who waits for no interruptions to any worship, implementation or religious programs. By worshiping Ganesha with devotion and devotion on Ganesh Chaturthi day, all evils will be removed and happiness, peace, knowledge and prosperity will be attained.

# CATEGORY: sri_krishna_janmashtami
## Shri Krishna Janmashtami

Lord Krishna appeared as the eighth son of Devaki-Vasudeva in the dungeon of Mathura at midnight in the auspicious yoga of Rohini Nakshatra on Shravana Bahula Ashtami.

The birthday of Lord Krishna, who showed the world the path of Dharma through education, polite protection and Bhagavad Gita, is celebrated with great enthusiasm across the country with fasting, vigil, listening to the story of Krishna Leela, arghya pradana and thugu Uyyale service.

# CATEGORY: paschima_jagara
## Paschim Jagar Puja

Paschim Jagar Puja is performed in the last yam of the night every day for a month, starting from the last yam of the night of Vijayadashami of Shuklapaksha of Ashwayuja month i.e. the morning dawn of Ekadashi, till the last yam of Kartika Shukla Ekadashi i.e. the dawn of Utthana Dwadashi. 'West' means last. It is called 'Paschima Jagar Pooja' because it is performed while awake (awake) during the last yam of the night.

Lord Vishnu is in yoga sleep during Chaturmasa for the protection of the world. These four months are like the four yamas of the night. The last month of these is the month of Kartika which is the last Java (Western Yama). Paschim Jagara is said to be the auspicious moment when Shri Lakshmi Devi warns Lord Narayana. On Asadha Shuddha Ekadashi, the Lord moves into Yoganidra. During this one month from Ekadashi of Ashwayuja month to Kartika Ekadashi, Lakshmidevi stands in front of the Supreme Lord in the form of lamp and aarti and warns the Supreme Lord (in the pre-sunrise yam) with extraordinary instrumental chants.

Some people also call it 'Pakshi Jagar Puja' as it is the time when the birds wake up early in the morning and start chirping. Classically it is also known as 'Prabodhotsava Puja'. On Brahmi Muhurta, one should get up, bathe, offer daily pooja to the house deity and light a special Neelanjana aarti. Aarti by ringing the conch and bells is a revival of Sanatan Dharma and the appeasement of evil forces in the atmosphere. After this pooja, the sweet mosaravalakki offered in the offering is distributed to the devotees.

On the night of Karthika Shuddha Chaturdashi, after returning to the temple from the town festival and offering service to Sri Varadaraja Swami Ashtaavadhaana, fruit offerings are made to Sri God by ten members of the town and prayers are performed for the success of the upcoming festival.

On Kartika Shuddha Purnima, Agrodaka is brought from Rudratheerth for the anointing of Shri God on Brahmi Muhurta. Then Pavamanabhisheka, Panchamritabhisheka, Mahanaivedya and MahaMangalaarati (three-time worship) are performed. Sri Varadaraja Swami, decked in Atta Prabhavali, goes out to the forest (Ambatirtha) on the full moon of Kartika along with the Samaj Bandhavar, as if "Bhakuta Jana is behind you".

Kalasa The field is surrounded by Panchatirthas, Ambathirtha has its own mythological and geographical features. Sri Venkataramana Temple Utsav Murthy is mesmerizing towards the Ambathirtha with chhatra, chamara, Mangalainstrument and impada bhajanavali. After abhisheka and puja, the sanctum sanctorum is sealed.

### Dhatri Havan (Amalaka Puja)
From time to time Dhatri Havan is held at Sri Chennakesava Temple and Vanabhojana at the temple. In the month of Kartika, Dhatri (gooseberry) tree is worshiped and worship of Kartika Damodar is done with shodashopacharas, jaggery, tovve, ghee with Dhatri namana mantra, homa etc.

* **Observation Time:** Vanabhojan is usually done on Kartika Shuddha Trayodashi, Chaturdashi, Purnima and Kartika Bahula Panchami. But on Sunday, Mangalaweek, Friday and Saptami, Dhatri Havan is not performed and gooseberry is not consumed. Fresh gooseberries are used only after the dhatri dedication to Srihari.
* **Place:** Havan should be done under the shade of Dhatri tree. If the tree is not available, the root portion of the Dhatri plant is brought and planted and worshipped. A mantapa is constructed from sugarcane, coconut and mango leaves and three peethas namely Vaikuntha, Anantasana and Shvetadweep are installed along with Sridevi-Bhudevi and Karthika Damodar is enshrined. For Dhatri Homa, 21 deities are worshiped mainly in the form of Lakshmi.
* **Significance and Benefit:** Participating in Vanabhojana removes Unda Dosha (Paranna/Dushtanna Dosha) in other's house and Vratalopa in Chaturmasya. Thousands of Ashwamedha yagas, hundreds of Vajpeya yagas and Ganga bath are available. Those who circumambulate at the time of the sacrifice attain sayujya with the paternal deities.
* **Medicinal properties:** Medicine called 'Dhatryarishta' is prepared from Dhatriphala. Panduroga, Jaundice and heart related problems are alleviated with this medicine made using gooseberry juice, honey and rock sugar.

On his return from the forest, Varadaraja Swami received many auspicious pujas and was enthroned in the Panchanga of the temple of Chennakesava Swami. After the evening Deevatige Salaam (Deepa Namaskara) is performed, **Harihara Samagam** takes place after midnight. The Jodi Utsav of Sri KalasaShwar and Sri Varadaraja Swami proceeds up to Sampige Katte, then returns to the temple after receiving Manjanakatte and Seven Katte pujas.

# CATEGORY: tulasi_vivaha
## Utthana Dwadashi - Tulsi Vivah

Srimannarayana, who goes into yogic sleep on Asadha Shuddha Ekadashi, awakens on Dwadashi of Kartika month. We celebrate that day as 'Utthana Dwadashi'. On this day, prayers are offered to Lord Damodar in a forest full of chuta (mango), ashwaththa, champaka, ala, dhatri (nelli) and tulsi.

**Legendary Background:** Tulsi has the name 'Vrinda'. Vrinda is the wife of Jalandhara, a demon. When no one is able to kill Jalandhara due to the influence of her chastity, Vrinde's chastity is broken when Vishnu comes disguised as Jalandhara and Jalandhara collapses. When Vrinda, knowing the matter, was on the pyre, Parvati built tulsi-nelli plants around the pyre and created Vrindavan, while Vishnu worshiped the tulsi. The same Vrinde was later born as Rukmini and married Lord Krishna on the day of Kartika Shuddha Dwadashi. The memory of this divine marriage festival is 'Utthana Dwadashi - Tulsee Vivah'.

# CATEGORY: ananta_chaturdashi
## Ananta Chaturdashi

**Legendary Background:** Lord Krishna suggests a plan to alleviate the sufferings of the Pandavas who were cheated by the Kauravas in a gambling game and lost their kingdom-luxuries and were in exile. He tells Dharmaraja to observe a fast for 14 consecutive days starting from the day of Ananta Chaturdashi and worship the form of Sri Ananta Padmanabha (Vishnu) with devotion. Yudhishthira diligently observes this vrata with his family and regains his lost honor and kingdom. Since then Ananta Chaturdashi is being celebrated as a holy festival.

# CATEGORY: ratha_saptami
## Ratha Saptami

Magha Shukla Saptami is celebrated as Rathasaptami. On this day, right before sunrise, if you take a morning bath at a river, confluence etc. and offer arghya to the sun, past sins and sorrows will be resolved. Long life, health and wealth are obtained.

**Legendary Glory:** There is a story that King Yashovarma's sick son was cured by Sun worship of Rathasaptami Vrata. The Pandavas worshiped the Sun in exile and got the Akshayapatra. Sri Rama had worshiped Surya through 'Adityahridaya' stotra as advised by Agastya to win over Ravana. Mayura Kavi created 'Suryashataka' and regained the sight of his lost eye.

## Mukkoti Dwadashi

It is believed that on Margashira Shuddha Dwadashi, three crore deities come to bathe in Sri Kshetra's Theerthas during Brahmi Muhurta. At such a holy time, after anointing Lord Shri, taking Tirtha bath will remove all sins.

On that day in Brahmi Muhurta, Sri Varadaraja Swami will return to Rudratheertha after receiving Bijayangaidu, Abhishek and Katte Pujas. Then Pavamanabhisheka, Panchamritabhisheka, Mahanaivedya, MahaMangalaarati and distribution of prasad to God and Varadaraja Swami will be done. At night Deva Pete Utsav takes place.
